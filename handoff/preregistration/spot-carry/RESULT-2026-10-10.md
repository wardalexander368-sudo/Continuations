**Does not hold.** Over lending USDC on Kamino, the carry made nothing reliable after costs.

* **Period A (Mar 16 - Jul 1), all 12 rules lost** against Kamino USDC: always-on −1.9% APR, the best (7-day signal,

* **Period B (Jul 1 - Oct 8), the A-chosen rule:** +0.8% APR [−2.2, +3.4] excess, and **2 liquidations** (PURR's perp

* Reported only: always-on in B was +6.0% APR [−0.9, +13.3] with the same 2 PURR liquidations; per coin, PUMP +17%,
  ZEC +12%, ENA +10%, ETH +4%, BTC +2%, HYPE +2%, SOL +1%, PURR −1%. Picking those coins now would be choosing on B.

**Bug fixed before the numbers above, disclosed:** the first run took a missing hourly spot candle (no trades that
hour) as "price unchanged" while the perp leg moved, which booked a naked short's P&L as carry (it showed +46% APR).
Prices are now the last traded close on both legs (`last_close` in `carry.py`). No rule, coin or cost changed.

Reading: on Hyperliquid's own spot pairs the funding floor is roughly what USDC lending pays, and the trade adds
bridge, venue and spike risk. Not a paper test. A variant worth a look later only if funding regimes change: higher
margin (1x) on the big bridged coins (BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE), where liquidation risk is low, but its excess here was
+1 to +4% APR before any risk charge.
