## Ranked list

1. **No US test can give a verdict in its 52 weeks: decide now what "good enough to go live small" means.**
   (Decision for AJ.) Every US registration needs a 90% CI lower bound above zero. CDE vol-momentum's test CI
   over 90 weeks was [−1.05, +1.94]%/wk; over 52 weeks the half-width is about ±1.7%/wk, so it passes only if
   the live mean is ~4x its backtest (+0.39). CAPIT fires on ~6 crash days a year (study: 28 trades on 11 days
   in 22 months), so its day-clustered CI cannot clear zero in a year either. VOLSPIKE (+1.3%/trade, ~30 days a
   year) is similar. So in October 2027 every US test will most likely read "inconclusive", whatever the truth.
   Suggested rule (not adopted; AJ's call): go live small on Alpaca for a strategy once its paper results sit
   inside its backtest's range after N events (e.g. 3 crash days for CAPIT), with a stake sized so the worst
   backtest stretch is a loss AJ accepts. Registrations stay as they are; this is a sizing decision, not a test.

2. **CAPIT is the strongest US signal and it holds on 10 extra US coins; VOLSPIKE does not.** New check
