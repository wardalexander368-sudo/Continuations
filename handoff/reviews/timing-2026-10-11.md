# Trade timing on the US strategies, 2026-10-11

Asked by AJ: "improving trade timings, such as signal-to-fill latency, accurately holding when earning
profit, and selling when it's more optimal". Scope: the three US-tradable paper strategies
(cde_vol_momentum, us_capit, us_volspike). The registered rules are unchanged; everything here is
research run on history with the projects' splits (train 2020-23, validation 2024, test 2025-01..2026-10),
one selection per family on train, test read once, Bonferroni beside the 90% CI. Entry lag on wick-bounce
and the dip rule is already settled (`drags-2026-10-11.md`) and not redone.

## 1. CDE vol-momentum: holding winners, entry lag, signal day

`experiments/edge-hunt/perps/cde_timing.py DAILY_DIR` (daily candles from `cde_forward.py`; reproduces the
registered rule exactly: train +0.51%/wk, val +1.08, test +0.39). Each variant as a paired weekly
difference to the registered rule, 8 variants:

| Variant | Train diff | Val diff | Turnover/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registered (quintile legs, Monday signal, Tuesday entry) | | | 1.21 |
| Keep a held coin while it ranks in the top/bottom 30% | +0.15% [-0.22,+0.54] | -0.59% [-2.27,+0.82] | 1.07 |
| ... top/bottom 40% | **+0.51% [-0.14,+1.17]** (best train) | -0.29% [-2.33,+1.49] | 0.94 |
| ... top/bottom 50% | -0.03% | +0.26% | 0.78 |
| Enter at the signal day's close (lag 0) | +0.30% [-0.35,+0.94] | +0.15% [-0.88,+1.21] | 1.21 |
| Enter 2 days after the signal | -0.21% | +0.46% | 1.21 |
| Signal on Wednesday / Friday / Sunday | -0.65 / -0.67 / -0.76% | +2.32 / +0.87 / -1.03% | 1.21 |

**Result: no change.** The best train variant (hold winners while in the top 40%) fails validation, so
test was not read. Holding winners longer saves ~0.27 legs of turnover a week (~0.1%/wk of cost at
20 bps) but the coins it keeps don't keep winning. Entering at the signal close is positive in both
train and validation but small, wasn't the train pick, and with daily data "the close" can't be hit by
an hourly agent anyway; noted, not acted on. Monday is no worse than other days in train.

## 2. US-spot patterns: signal-to-fill latency

`experiments/edge-hunt/patterns/timing.py CACHE_DIR` (Coinbase 15-minute candles 2020-01..2026-10 from
`fetch_coinbase.py`, the registered signals, net 0.39%/side = Alpaca). The hourly agent starts at :21-:32
(median :22; autotrade journal 10-10/11), so the registered entry candle (closing :30) is usually not final
when the run sees the signal: the paper book is filled at the :30 price on the next run, and a real order
sent then (the Alpaca mirror) fills ~80-90 minutes after the signal. Entry and exit both D minutes late,
all splits pooled (per-split rows in the script output):

| Delay | CAPIT Z=4 H=24 (105 trades) | VOLSPIKE K=10 H=72 (248 trades) |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min (the first run after the signal) | **+4.11%/trade** [+1.10, +7.10] | +1.56% [-0.00, +3.16] |
| 30 min (registered) | +2.97% [+0.28, +5.56] | +1.45% [-0.14, +3.09] |
| 60 min | +2.61% | +1.45% |
| 90 min (an order at the next run) | **+1.93%** [-0.96, +4.41] | +1.43% [-0.18, +3.12] |
| 120 min | +2.19% | +1.37% |

**Capitulation bounces fade fast**: each split is best at 15 minutes (train +1.79 / val +5.43 / test +7.56
vs +0.78 / +4.30 / +6.23 registered), and waiting for the next run gives back ~1 point a trade, a third of
the edge. Volume spikes don't care. This is a monotone latency effect, not a parameter pick, but the
capit sample is small (105 trades, crash days cluster).

**Done:** `coinbase_pattern` takes `delay_bars` (default 2 = registered) and a paper research line
`us_capit_fast` (delay 1: booked at the :15 close by the first run after the signal, exit likewise) runs
beside the registered `us_capit`, which is unchanged. **Proposed, not done (owners decide):** if a live
capit route is ever built, it should send its order at the first run after the signal, not after the
:30 candle; the Alpaca mirror (`alpaca_paper.strategies`) could follow `us_capit_fast` instead of
`us_capit` so its measured fills match the timing a live route would use. Caveat: a run that starts
after :31 still books the :15 price, slightly flattering the fast line.

## 3. US-spot patterns: exits (holding winners, selling better)

Same script, 24 exit variants (12 per rule), each decided at hourly looks only and scored as a paired
difference to the registered fixed hold on the same entries: other fixed holds (H/2, 2H, 3H), trailing
stops at 1/2/3 daily sigmas, take-profit at 1/2 sigmas, a break-even stop, and three "hold on while
winning" rules (extend while net in profit, while the last 24 h is up, or switch to a trail once in profit).

* **CAPIT:** every variant but one loses to the 24 h hold in train; the best (take profit at +1 sigma,
  +0.35%) fails validation (-1.80%). Trailing stops cost 2-3 points a trade, "hold while in profit" -3.05%.
  Test not read.
* **VOLSPIKE:** the train pick, holding 216 h instead of 72, is +1.64% train / +5.75% val but **-1.60% on
  test** [-3.79, +0.71] (Bonferroni [-5.56, +2.45]). "Hold on while in profit" is +1.55 / +2.32 but wasn't
  the pick; with the 216 h result as a guide, read as noise. Trails and take-profits all lose in train.

**Result: keep the registered fixed holds.** Selling into strength or trailing cuts the bounces that pay;
holding longer did not survive test. Together with the closed launch-exit grids, stops and trails have now
failed on every rule we have.

## Bottom line

Exits are fine as registered. The one timing fix that pays is entering the capitulation bounce at the first
run after the signal: about +1 point a trade against what the current next-run timing gets. It now runs
as a paper research line; the registered tests are untouched.
