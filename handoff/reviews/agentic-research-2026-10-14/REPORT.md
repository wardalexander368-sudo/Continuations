# Agentic trading research: what to retain, change and test

14 October 2026. Review of153 supplied PDF pages plus bounded primary-source and version checks. This report records source-based learning and project planning; no paper's returns were replicated and no new return screen, trade, account call or method-source collection was performed. The user explicitly authorized retaining principles and realigning this project's research priorities. Existing main-review, paper-only, US-only, owner-book, frozen-registration and excluded-project boundaries remain.

## Decision

Keep the goal of an incremental, feasible, US-expressible net trading edge. Change the immediate emphasis to finishing one auditable economic qualification/falsification path. Our missing inputs are timely complete observations, applicable costs/units, executable quotes/fills and matched full-account evidence. Another agent architecture is not a substitute for those inputs.

We should retain source/version fidelity, explicit missingness, causal memory, deterministic account authority, typed action semantics, all-in research costs, independent-event inference and simple attributable comparisons. The report delivers a45-item backlog, an operating charter, a planning card and one composition-volume proposal. Those are documentation/planning changes; their existence does not implement their runtime checks or validate alpha.

The adopted small-size cost-measurement policy is an owner sizing decision, distinct from a positive-alpha finding. This review does not amend its numeric gates or interpret a gate pass as profitability.

No supported parameter optimization follows from these papers. SLR-0.2 remains unvalidated, CAPIT/adopted rules remain unchanged, closed memecoin live lanes stay closed, and actual account profitability remains unknown.

## What each source teaches

### Lin: participant composition is a question, not a wallet label

The setup has40 agents,5 synthetic stocks,15 rounds and8 reported composition runs (PDF pp.17,33,39). Fundamentalist/contrarian personas are grouped as rationalists. Real wallets have no such observed persona ground truth. Repeated buying does not identify an independent person, informed trader, profitable account or future supporter.

The reported600 stock-round rows reduce to560 for a one-lead model and520 for differenced/lagged models (pp.40,43,49). Assets within a run share agents, capital and a forum. Forty stock/run panels are not forty independent markets, and600 rows are not600 independent investment trials. Report uncertainty at the actual common-shock/event level and retain complete attempted-run selection history.

The next-price model's table includes a lagged-price term omitted from its displayed equation. Its reported volume slope is -0.212 + 0.644R (p.40); the implied synthetic crossing near 0.329 is not a real-wallet threshold. The price-change model includes lower-order interactions omitted from the abbreviated equation (p.43); a positive volume slope does not automatically mean momentum. Outcomes are levels/absolute changes, not net executable returns. Near-zero volume/persona-share correlation(p.48) cannot establish independence or no confounding.

The simulator's salary depends on recent P&L(pp.22-23), so terminal wealth mixes trading, external transfers and marks. The text alternates linear and square-root impact(pp.24-29), lacks reproducible per-run calibration and has default-action/random-upvote fallbacks on failures(pp.31-32). We retain the mechanisms as hypotheses and preserve these limitations. We do not copy impact coefficients, rationalist thresholds or wealth curves into fees, wallet scores or alpha claims.

Useful project extensions are observable exact-mint buyer breadth, notional concentration, history completeness, original social breadth and strict first-observed chronology. Existing97.222% returning-buyer and87.354% largest-buyer shares describe one explored episode; they cannot set optimized cutoffs or supply independent validation. A feature may be descriptively correct while having no useful predictive increment.

### Aigner: use a map only after checking its claims

The survey usefully lists DRL, hybrid, LLM, multi-agent and simulation approaches and themes such as memory, tools, budgets, risk and latency. These describe different dimensions, not an empirical performance ranking. We should add source/action/rights/economics fields rather than select a framework by stars.

Several important claims fail source checks. Aigner says no joint reward-based LLM/RL optimization exists(pp.3,8), but [FLAG-Trader's published method](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.716/) uses a trainable LLM policy. Its quoted FLAG universe/windows and TradingAgents period/summary metrics do not match the checked original experiments. The source review records exact comparisons and labels unlocated percentages unverified rather than false by assumption.

The cited [KTD-Fin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28359v1) concerns historical-memory leakage and return attribution, not the survey's stated RAG saturation thresholds. This motivates a leakage/attribution question, not a large memory build. The survey's API/training/latency table(p.8) is explicitly estimated; it supplies no metered workload or current price quote. We need our own calls/tokens/retries/time/dollar receipts, with unknown costs visible.

Useful features are typed information tools, cached evidence with provenance, separated research/review roles, deterministic risk limits, lower-cost baselines and measured resource/latency budgets. Defer training, online adaptation, large RAG and always-on multi-agent trading until they solve a measured problem under an equal-resource ablation. Consensus, reflection and plausible rationales are not independent market evidence.

### Xia: make every claimed system improvement auditable

The v1 map has92 candidate records,77 included(19 primary + 58 background) and15 excluded. Its primary boundary is action output plus a closed-loop evaluation; a backtest or simulator can qualify without broker fills. In the fixed 19,2 have extractable splits,1 explicit costs,1 universe handling and11 execution semantics. R-tier counts15/1/3/0 sum correctly(pp.45-46). These are the authors' reported extraction/coding facts; missing reporting is not proof safeguards were absent. R tiers describe artifact readiness, not economic validity, rights or actual replay by us.

The full per-study extraction and agreement sheets were not available in the supplied PDF. One illustrative table says20 works but displays 19, with 15 rather than 17 recent rows(pp.44-45). Section-level 9 versus 12 coordination counts remain ambiguous; a portfolio BG/cost shorthand can conflict with the main eligibility definition if applied globally. These issues reinforce separating scope, missingness, replay and economic claims in our own card.

Most useful designs: keep immutable account truth separate from generated context(p.10); filter memories and reflections by actual availability(pp.10-12); define observation-to-intent-to-order-to-fill/account contracts(pp.16-18); fix cost/execution semantics across adaptive updates(pp.34-38); and report data, splits, actions, costs, leakage, artifacts and relevant adaptation details(MR-1 through MR-7, pp.38-39). The checklist is author-proposed, not a standard or measured profit improvement. We choose stricter applicable evidence requirements for claims we intend to rely on.

## Strategy optimization that is justified to investigate

[The composition-volume proposal](../../research/COMPOSITION-VOLUME-PROPOSAL.md) has two stages. First verify descriptive features from eligible saved evidence: gross-debit buyer shares, HHI/effective address count, bounded returning-history labels and original social breadth. Then, only if full prerequisites are met, a separate fixed prospective test asks whether one composition interaction adds net-dollar value beyond a volume/price/liquidity baseline with the same account/coverage constraints. It cannot reuse the selected episode as untouched data, infer wallet intelligence or reopen the closed memecoin live lane.

Possible later improvements to compare are lower turnover/no-trade bands, latency-aware feasibility, matched exposure controls and selective deterministic retrieval. None is justified as a current strategy change by these documents; each would be another trial under the complete search history. There was no new return simulation here, no new optimized parameter and no demonstrated profit uplift.

## What is retained and what remains open

The user authorized a small persistent learning note plus repository principles. Retain methods for evaluating claims and learning; do not memorize unverified return numbers, disputed RAG thresholds or temporary source/reporting counts as truths. Dated findings should always carry version and limits. Cash/account authority, failed-source history, frozen windows and reviewer responsibility survive chat summaries.

Open: explicit PR16 receipt-egress approval; Claude final reviews/merges; owner understanding of permanent coverage qualification; actual costs/units/quotes/fills and an attainable untouched cohort; optional full v2/per-study-ledger audit only if a concrete decision needs it. Desired capital, drawdown tolerance and research-spend ceiling must be explicit before any candidate requiring them is finalized; this report does not invent those preferences.

No account/trading/purchase authority was expanded. No supplied PDF/foreign code was executed. The exhausted source/screen allowances were preserved and no additional collector was created. This review establishes learning and a reviewable plan, not a profitable strategy.
