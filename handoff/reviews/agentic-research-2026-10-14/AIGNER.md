# Independent review: Aigner agentic-trading survey

Reviewed 14 October 2026. This is a source audit and proposed process map, not an implemented policy, trading signal, return simulation or profitability finding.

## Source, provenance and review method

**Attached source:** `Aigner-Agentic-Trading-Overwview-Jun-2026.pdf`, 12 PDF pages, 229,322 bytes, SHA-256 `21302f3d7cf66c8bd377b043979e6d09a0a0282d0df6269e874912ae81d68448`. The PDF itself says it was written independently and is unaffiliated with reviewed projects (p.11).

**Classification:** a broad independent narrative survey and source-discovery aid. It mixes research papers, repositories, YouTube/web material, citation/star counts, commercial observations and author estimates. It provides no experiment of its own, no audited broker account, no reproducible corpus ledger, and no evidence that our account can earn the quoted returns. Its claim of examining more than 60 papers/frameworks/repos (pp.1-2) is not the same as 60 comparable economic experiments; only 40 references are numbered, and no selection/exclusion flow or per-claim extraction table is supplied.

The primary-paper checks below used official arXiv and ACL Anthology pages/PDFs. They were document checks, not reruns of the papers. No GitHub method snapshot, collector, account, paid API, order or return screen was used. The exhausted intake allowance was neither consumed through the collector nor reset. Reading an original paper does not approve its associated code, weights, data or license for reuse.

**Primary-check identity limit:** checks were performed on2026-10-14. Some arXiv links below are unversioned; the exact returned revision and remote PDF hash were not retained. ## Full page-by-page extraction of useful content

**Page1: purpose and scope.** The promise is a map of LLM/RL trading systems and ten research gaps. Useful learning: identify the entire decision loop and the evidence needed at each boundary. Limitation: the abstract already conflates adoption, innovation and economic performance; it supplies neither an independent experiment nor a systematic evidence denominator.

**Page2: five categories and comparison axes.** Categories are DRL, LLM-augmented DRL, LLM decision-makers, multi-agent systems and simulation environments. Comparison axes include input modalities, memory, learning, risk, explainability, deployment and latency. Useful as separate metadata fields in a candidate card. They are not mutually exclusive evidence tiers: a simulator is an evaluation setting, a multi-agent system can use RL, and an explainable text answer is not an executable result. Add venue eligibility, precise action semantics, cost basis, available-at chronology, artifact identity and validation status, which matter more to our promotion decision.

**Page3: FinRL, DRL constraints, risk sensitivity and hybrid patterns.** Modular acquisition/environment/policy separation is useful. The warnings about sample inefficiency, overfitting and changing regimes align with our preference for cheap deterministic baselines. CVaR and stress-risk reporting can be descriptive additions if sample size is disclosed. The DAPO, Decision Transformer and FinRL family are source leads, not reasons to train or buy compute. The FLAG mechanism/performance summary must be corrected as above.

**Page4: reflection, memory, profiles and role specialization.** Distinguish model training, in-context experience and ordinary retrieval; none is automatically a statistically controlled learning update. Retain evidence retrieval with origin, availability and expiry. A source-backed summary can help a researcher remember why an idea was rejected. A narrative risk personality must not alter cash limits or strategy parameters silently. The claimed memory uplift and TradingAgents headline are not established by this survey.

**Page5: rationality prompts, memory control and tools.** Adopt typed information retrieval, deterministic numeric tools, tool-output provenance and explicit abstention on unavailable data. Counterfactual review is useful when it asks whether a signal beats matched alternatives. Do not equate a persuasive counterfactual story or debiasing prompt with an economic control. The KTD-Fin citation needs the substantive correction above. Broker API capability does not authorize sending orders.

**Page6: coordination, portfolio balance, simulations and evaluation expense.** Separate evidence gathering, method transcription, skeptical review, accounting and promotion ownership. Shared structured records are preferable to long free-form agent conversations. Debate among agents is not independent market evidence. Compare the minimum adequate single-process baseline with any coordination proposal under the same resource budget. Behavioral market simulations are useful for software stress tests; their synthetic profits do not establish a tradable real-world edge. The LOB and endogenous-impact leads need calibration and venue data before deployment claims.

**Page7: benchmarks and sim-to-real gap.** The strongest useful content is size/liquidity limits, stale data, latency, queue uncertainty and path dependence. These agree with our passive-grid preflight: saved candles cannot verify limit fills. Task/QA benchmarks can evaluate extraction or arithmetic reliability, but not net trading alpha. Live feeds do not by themselves reconcile broker fills or complete account economics. Tool/agent versions and repeated benchmark runs need a trial ledger.

**Page8: commercial comparison, economics and optimization.** Treat commercial capability statements as dated unverified context, not vendor recommendations or venue-access guidance. Visually checked Table1 lists GPT-4o API cost $200-$2,000/month, DeepSeek $30-$300, hybrid $100-$1,000 plus $500-$5,000 training, PPO $0 API plus $50-$500 training, and four-agent $500-$8,000 plus $0-$2,000 training. Its estimates omit a metered workload and current prices. The arithmetic matters: at a hypothetical $1,000 account, $200 or $500/month alone require 20% or 50% incremental monthly return merely to cover that line item. That is an illustration of the budget burden, not a profit forecast. Use measured all-in marginal cost and caching before considering a paid inference architecture. The end-to-end novelty claim conflicts with cited primary work.

**Page9: regimes, impact, risk metrics, consistency, governance and security.** Preserve the checklist themes, but avoid adopting every suggested mechanism. Regime detection is a hypothesis that itself needs a fixed rule and untouched evaluation; adding adaptive prompts after a drawdown is another trial. Report drawdown duration, downside/tail risk and costs only with correct units, finite values and adequate independent observations. Strategy commitment should be enforced by method/config hashes and deterministic constraints. Untrusted news, PDFs and repositories are data, not authority to expand tools or change instructions. Human-readable reasons require evidence references; they do not reveal a model's true internal reasoning. The SR11-7 discussion is not legal advice or a current regulatory determination.

**Page10: latency, transfer, configuration and conclusions.** Measure retrieval-to-decision and decision-to-fill latency distributions instead of borrowing 1-15s. Keep strategic research asynchronous; deterministic validation/accounting should remain outside any slow model call. Cross-market comparisons require separate product units, calendars, trading rights, costs and constraints. A natural-language request can compile to a reviewed method/protocol proposal, never directly to an order. The claim that hybrids offer the best performance/reliability is premature under the reporting gaps.

**Page11: outlook, independence and references1-23.** Useful bibliography, particularly primary framework, memory and tool orchestration leads. Forecasts about the next three years or first regulatory approval are speculative. A scholarly-looking reference list and affiliation do not replace extracting the actual experiment. Track version/date and author-correct source; the AlphaQuanter ACL publication is verifiable, but broader validity remains untested here.

**Page12: references24-40.** Useful simulation, benchmark, consistency and security leads. Several citations omit an identifier or complete publication metadata. Resolve exact artifacts before extraction; don't launch a collector against guessed names or treat absent IDs as validated papers. Related AMA versions should be linked to avoid inflated evidence counts.

## Relation to the third, audit-oriented survey

The provided *Agentic Trading: When LLM Agents Meet Financial Markets*, arXiv:2605.19337v1, p.1 and its evidence-ledger discussion, gives a much stricter denominator: 77 included studies, but only 19 satisfy action output plus closed-loop evaluation; the remaining 58 are background/design context. In those 19, it extracts time-consistent splits from 2, an explicit cost model from 1, universe/survivorship handling from 1, execution semantics from 11; 15 are R0 and none R3. These are **reporting/coding findings within that chosen subset**, not proof that exactly those proportions apply to the entire literature, and missing reported evidence does not prove an author never used a control. Its search snapshot ends 9 March 2026; later studies such as KTD-Fin cannot be retroactively counted in its denominator. [Official abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19337v1).

Aigner ranks sophistication/adoption and repeatedly says architectures significantly improve performance; the audit survey says protocols are often not comparable. For our decisions the audit-oriented lens should govern. Keep Aigner's categories as discovery tags, then require closed-loop action semantics, chronology, costs, universe controls and reproducibility before taking economic claims seriously. Human-readable rationale or many specialized agents is a capability feature, not an evidence upgrade. The stronger survey does not itself validate any strategy for us.

## Reproducible improvements worth building first

1. **Source-claim ledger alongside source-code intake.** A small schema/markdown table suffices: claim ID, source/version/hash, PDF page/table, reported metric/unit/window/universe, cost/venue assumptions, verification status, contradiction note and candidate links. Test with the known FLAG, TradingAgents and KTD-Fin mismatches above. No network or new return screen is needed for that fixture. Keep paper proof distinct from implementation/license proof.
2. **Evidence contract before any LLM component.** Define typed read-only observation, tool-call, action-proposal and rejection records. Deterministic finite/cash/chronology checks own authority. Add synthetic tests for future observations, unavailable tool data, injected instructions, duplicate calls, nonfinite sizes and stale-memory retrieval. No autonomous trading component is required.
3. **Incremental-dollar and resource report.** Existing cached screen/account outputs can disclose net dollars, benchmark excess, drawdown, turnover, concentration, estimated execution costs and measured research overhead. Unknowns stay unknown. Pricey inference needs a declared incremental-value hypothesis and a call/time/dollar ceiling before deployment testing. The survey's budget estimates are not defaults.
4. **Memory provenance as ordinary data engineering.** Version summaries; link every lesson to observed receipts; keep failure/closed-lane rationale; assign usable-at/expiry and superseded state. Prevent retrieval of later outcomes into earlier decisions. Start with deterministic local lookup rather than a vector database or extra model calls.
5. **Optional future masked/attribution evaluation protocol.** Before using an LLM to claim historical selection skill, document train/pretraining contamination risk, uniform identifier/date masking, identical signals/tool outputs and same-capital risk/exposure-matched controls. Preserve one untouched prospective cohort. This is a future protocol proposal, not permission to spend today's consumed screen allowance.

These are own controls informed by literature, not copies of third-party implementation. They can improve reliability and reduce wasted research cost without any promise of increased returns.

## Things to defer and why

- **Training PPO/DAPO/Decision Transformers, end-to-end LLM/RL, or distillation:** no reliable source-specific incremental-alpha evidence or adequate untouched data here; training adds selection and compute costs.
- **Always-on multi-agent analyst/risk/trader debates:** no equal-budget ablation, known cost multiplier and correlated model errors. Existing role-specific PR review is useful without putting agents in the order path.
- **Large RAG memories or dynamic risk personalities:** the paper's quoted saturation/uplift numbers are unverified or misattributed; mutable memories can leak outcomes and silently retune a strategy.
- **Automatic regime filters, adaptive prompts, online policy updates:** they create new trials and can invalidate frozen evidence. First quantify regime coverage descriptively.
- **Full ABIDES/TwinMarket/StockAgent market worlds:** potentially useful for specific stress mechanisms, but calibration, microstructure evidence and validation costs come before any return claim.
- **Passive grid return testing on the present closes:** unresolved funding/execution prerequisites remain. This literature does not supply the missing quotes or prove fills.
- **Commercial subscriptions or paid APIs:** marketing comparisons are not procurement evidence. No account access, purchase or subscription is needed to make the proposed controls reviewable.
- **Portfolio leverage/derivatives/foreign-venue transfer:** no change to US eligibility or paper-only constraints follows from an architecture survey.

## Proposed retained learning principles and goal framing

These are proposals for the project's own principles document. No persistent memory or project policy was edited.

- Optimize distance to a reproducible, US-expressible, adequately powered **incremental net edge**. More agents, frameworks, stars, model sophistication and research activity are not progress by themselves.
- A claim graduates through source verification, mechanism fidelity, feasible product/accounting, chronological execution evidence, matched controls and untouched evaluation. Each step can fail honestly.
- Separate architecture capability, software correctness, benchmark performance and economically validated alpha. Passing engineering checks can improve trust without proving profit.
- Store what was knowable when, what was actually attempted, and what failed. Unknown and NOT_SCREENABLE are productive outputs when they identify the next necessary evidence.
- Prefer the cheapest deterministic system that answers the current research question. An LLM earns a role only through a declared equal-budget ablation and measured incremental value.
- Treat learning as a versioned change to evidence or policy. Updating a summary is not permission to retune a frozen strategy; adaptation needs its own prospective protocol.
- Ground explanations in cited observations and deterministic calculations. Model confidence, self-reflection and consensus are hypotheses about behavior, not independent evidence.
- Keep irreversible execution authority outside the research agent. Continue paper-only, bounded read-only collection and the existing review/merge gates.

A possible explicit objective is: **Find a feasible strategy whose prospective, exposure-matched dollar advantage remains positive after all execution and operating costs, with acceptable drawdown and an independently reviewable record.** Desired capital, drawdown tolerance, evaluation horizon and monthly research-cost ceiling remain user preferences to confirm; this survey cannot choose them. No validated alpha, eligibility change, threshold change or novelty claim follows from this review.

## Limits of this review

This review did not verify all 40 references, current star/citation counts, commercial capabilities, current legal requirements, actual broker fills, repository licenses or any paper's executable implementation. It did not reproduce reported returns, run benchmark models, acquire method snapshots, change frozen or owner state, access CTS, reset budgets, create orders or alter memory. Primary-source links support specific document/architecture checks, not endorsements or profitability validation. The arXiv HTML rendition of TradingAgents showed anomalous author text; experimental-scope and table comparisons above use its PDF instead. The attached Aigner PDF remains the source being reviewed, with exact pages retained for every material recommendation.
