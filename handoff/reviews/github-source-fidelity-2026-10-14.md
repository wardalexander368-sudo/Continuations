## Why the grid needs a different test

The pinned [balancer source](https://github.com/caktux/pytrader/blob/b45b216dab3db78d6028d85e9a6f80419c22cea0/balancer.py) keeps a 50/50 allocation formula (lines320–338), but places two standing orders around cash/base-derived center (300–318,340–349). Defaults sell at center×1.05 and buy at center/1.05 before fee compensation (21–22,624–655). A reciprocal buy step is not a symmetric five-percent fall. Filled orders trigger balance refresh, cancel and replacement (553–592); daily market rebalancing lacks that lifecycle.

Both order sizes are forcibly increased to at least0.1base units (396–402). For this audit's BTC/USD interpretation, the saved2024 start close is **$42,452.66**. One0.1BTC order has a $4,245.27 nominal value there. Holding0.1BTC in a50% initial allocation requires at least **$8,490.53 total capital before fees**. A $1,000 account has only 0.01177782BTC at that starting close. All8,784selected hourly closes are above $5,000; the smallest corresponding50%-allocation funding floor is $7,741.25. These are static arithmetic bounds on saved closes, not executable quotes, capital advice or current venue lot rules.

Source code supports several trading currencies, cold holdings, reserve balances, forced price files and an external configuration. Its actual operator configuration is unknown. Literal simulated code defaults of quote15/base5000 are not our $1,000 balanced protocol. The 0.1clamp is an old source behavior, not a verified present-day US product minimum. A fractional-order US adaptation could be investigated only as an explicit new method/protocol, with its own executable product and cost evidence.

The old simulated fee default is0.05%=5bp (32,47), while our earlier screen declared39/78bp per side. Source fee compensation adjusts limit prices using two factors of(1−fee), and quote corrections can move sell to ask×1.01 or buy to bid×0.99 (353–391,594–622). Those settings are separate from recording actual paid fees.

The source simulation credits a full sell on an ask touch and full buy on a bid touch (434–448). It deducts a fee from received base on buys, but its sell wallet update contains no explicit fee debit. Neither touch proves fills or queue priority. These observations concern the inspected simulator, not a reproduced defect or profit result in its live exchange implementation. Do not copy those assumptions into our ledger.

An honest passive-grid test needs ordered bid/ask and trades, submissions/acknowledgements, partial/full fills, cancellations and balance updates, paid-fee denomination, product units/precision, gaps and receipt times. Hourly close/volume does not supply them. OHLC alone cannot determine the order of several crossings or establish queue fills. Hash-present files establish identity, not semantic execution proof.
