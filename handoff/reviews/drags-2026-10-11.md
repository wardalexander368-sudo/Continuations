# Drags and lags in our trading, 2026-10-11

Asked by AJ ("focus on decreasing drags and lags in our trading"). Each drag was measured before
deciding on a fix. Short version: the lags we were worried about cost nothing measurable, the
perps fee saving from maker orders is small at go-live size, and one live-only drag (the JLP hedge
rebalance) is real but can't bite until that basket has a live route. Nothing in the agent changed
in this pass; the earlier fetch budget (d53c531) already keeps the hourly run inside its timeout.

| Drag | Measured cost | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Hourly runs start late (cron :05) | Starts 14-26 min after :05 (5 runs, 2026-10-10/11); other workflows at odd minutes start 8-20 min late | None: no strategy loses money to it (rows below) |
| Wick-bounce enters ~20 min after the wick hour | **+17 bp/trade, 90% CI [-4, +39]** vs entering at the close (409 trades 2023-01..2026-09); +12 bp at 10 min | None: late entry doesn't hurt, the bounce keeps going |
| Dip entry at :20 instead of the signal | ~0 (replay: -3.94% vs -3.98% gross at 30 min late) | None (and the dip rule is closed for real money) |
| Perps taker fees (4.5 bps + fill) vs maker (1.5 bps) | Turnover ~1.15-1.2 of a leg per week; 6 → 1.5 bps saves ~0.10% of a leg per week, **about $1/week** for both books at ~$500 a leg | Not now: post-only orders need repost and partial-fill handling for $50/yr. Worth it if the account grows 10x |
| Delay between signal and order inside a run | Perps orders wait behind the dip fetch (35 s to a few min); price moves over minutes have ~0 mean | None |
| **JLP hedge rebalance, live path** | Paper charges the taker fee on the changed size only; the live twins close every short and reopen it, ~2 x 0.64 x 6 bps ≈ **7.7 bps of the basket per rebalance** vs ≈0.4 bps on paper | Fix before the basket goes live: resize the twins by the difference. Can't happen today: the basket's JLP leg needs the Jupiter wallet route, which isn't set up |

## How each was measured

* **Start lag:** `created_at` of every scheduled run on GitHub vs its cron minute. autotrade: 20:23,
  22:19, 23:18, 00:31, 01:20 (cron :05). health (:45): :55-:04. pumpflow-paper (:15): :23-:35.
  hl-book (:17): :25-:36. perps-ctx (:52): :00-:06. paper-tests (:40): :48-:58. Top-of-hour jobs
  queue longest, but moving autotrade's minute only pays if some strategy loses money to the lag,
  and none does.
* **Wick-bounce:** `handoff/experiments/edge-hunt/perps/wick_entry_lag.py` (Binance 1h for signals,
  5m for prices, the registered rule and costs). Mean net per trade by minutes after the hour, entry
  and exit both late: 0: +47 bp, 5: +66, 10: +58, 15: +68, 20: +63, 30: +73, 60: +38. On 2025-01..2026-09
  (125 trades) the 20-minute entry is +107 vs +79. The audit's "-137 → -236 bp at 1 h late" came from
  29 HL trades in a bad period; on 409 trades an hour late costs ~9 bp. The agent's execution-realistic
  score (entry at :20) stays as is: it matches what we measured.
* **Perps fees:** `tartar/autotrade/search.py backtest` on the agent's own panel, cost 0 vs 10 bps.
  xs_momentum: gross +1.49%/wk per $1 a leg, turnover 1.15 a week; at 6 bps/side net +1.35%, at
  1.5 bps +1.45%. vol_momentum: +1.90% gross, turnover 1.20; +1.76% vs +1.87%. Maker fills aren't
  guaranteed and fills that wait get picked off, so the real saving is below this upper bound.
* **JLP rebalance:** `tartar/autotrade/strategies/jlp_hedged.py` closes every short and reopens it
  on a rebalance (fee charged on `off[c]` only); the broker mirrors each close and open as a full
  twin trade. Hedge ≈ 0.64 of the JLP value (snapshots). Hedge drift so far is ~0.5% of JLP value
  a day against a 5% threshold, so a rebalance comes every few weeks. At 1-2 a month the live
  drag is ~1-2% APR against the test's +7.9% APR exploratory edge.
