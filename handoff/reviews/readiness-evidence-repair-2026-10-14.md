A fully filled sell request alone does not prove closure of its buy. Nonfinite accumulated quantities, book/held dollar values, differences or tolerances remain unconfirmed. Existing finite dust/tolerance values and economic strategy constants remain unchanged. Older unchecked reconciliations and missing clock/completion fields remain unknown. Idempotently recovered submissions may legitimately predate the current local run; that fact alone is not rejected as a contradiction.

Earlier eligible hours are catch-up/incomplete, even if the event time lies in the registered window. The returned response observation clock is taken after the HTTP operation. Local observation time is never relabeled as broker fill time. Same-run reconciliation may precede a completed response observation, requiring a later fresh reconciliation before readiness.
