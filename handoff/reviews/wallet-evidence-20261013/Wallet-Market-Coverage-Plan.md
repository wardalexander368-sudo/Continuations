# Market and buyer evidence plan — 2026-10-13 UTC

The active goal is to investigate profitable methods across the connected Coin Hunt projects and use observed market behavior to develop testable combinations. This wallet package extends descriptive buyer evidence. It supplies neither a profitable-wallet ranking nor an untouched strategy result. SLR-0.2 remains unvalidated.

A quiet four-hour read-only follow-up now watches for meaningful evidence and review changes. It should reuse existing observations, report failures and gaps, and notify when a change permits a concrete next step. It is not a continuous trade/social collector or proof that every relevant market dimension is covered.

All dimensions need a coverage status alongside their metric: available, partial, stale, failed or unknown. An absent event, unavailable account, empty response or missing execution quote must not silently become a zero. Wallet address, fee payer, router, creator address and human owner remain separate roles. Current token quantity is a quantity, not an asset valuation or a realized return.

The archive-to-paper join uses only exact `meta.mint`. Historical overlap is useful for identifying assets and wallets to observe prospectively; it does not backfill buyers at paper entry. Paper and shadow variants remain paper, and correlated variants are not independent executions. Historical evidence gathered after entry cannot be promoted to information known at entry.

## Candidate combinations to test prospectively

These are research questions derived from the observed gaps, not validated rules or claims that nobody has tried them. Before a claim of originality, search primary papers, public strategy descriptions and the project’s own prior decisions, preserving the search terms and dates.

1. **Concentration, returner flow and executable depth.** Test whether high returner volume predicts continuation only when concentration is lower and executable exit depth is sufficient. Compare against size/liquidity/volume-matched controls; unknown buyer history stays a separate category.
2. **Route cost and buyer flow.** Compare native-funded, stablecoin-routed and other-mint-funded buys at matched sizes and liquidity. Measure complete debit, route delay and failure/no-fill cost before asking whether a route-adjusted flow signal has value.
3. **Infrastructure-adjusted demand.** Examine whether distinct-wallet flow loses explanatory power when grouped by observed shared fee payer or routing source. Treat these as infrastructure groups; do not relabel them as common human owners.
4. **Inventory change around concentration shocks.** Observe full target-token acquisition/transfer/disposal paths before and after a dominant buyer arrives. Test whether costed persistence differs from an otherwise comparable independent-wallet influx. A current balance snapshot alone cannot supply this trajectory.
5. **Social evidence with chronological wallet context.** Evaluate exact-mint social activity alongside pre-entry wallet behavior, keeping independently timestamped receipts and late arrivals. Compare each addition against the same trading horizon, cost model and matched market controls.
6. **Signal economics across execution methods.** For an authorized future simulation, compare immediate execution against a fixed passive window and fallback policy over every eligible signal. Include zero/partial fills, delayed recoveries, taker fallback, capital lockup and complete fees; do not compare only successful maker fills.

The existing historical cache may support exploratory diagnostics and feasibility estimates. It cannot serve as an untouched holdout after this exploration. Choose a small set of questions before collecting a new evaluation cohort, lock their definitions and exclusions, and preserve the existing strategy registrations. Any new candidate needs a separate explicit protocol.

## Evidence needed before a profitability claim

For buyer-conditioned memecoin/SLR studies, use an untouched cohort with continuous exact-mint social and chain coverage, chronological buyer evidence, intended-size executable entry/exit quotes and matched full-account controls. For US CAPIT and other CEX strategies, require their own untouched chronological market data, executable quotes/costs or reconciled fills, and account controls appropriate to the method; exact-mint social coverage is not a universal prerequisite. Retain all eligible signals, unsuccessful routes, missing sources and unresolved costs. Separate estimated fees, simulated fills and reconciled executions. Report net dollars per eligible signal, exposure and capital constraints alongside returns; preserve uncertainty across market episodes and connected coin shocks.

For wallet-level profitability, first recover complete asset-specific opening inventory, acquisitions, disposals, transfers and payer costs. Partial swap sums and current balances cannot establish realized PnL, win rate, net worth or a profitable trader. The next useful collection is the missing chronological evidence, rather than another ranking based on the already selected seven wallets.
