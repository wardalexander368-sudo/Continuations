# Research evidence and change control

Implemented from Alex's 2026-10-13 instruction to build the page/article review's
improvements. This governs research records and interpretation; it does not
amend any frozen primary rule, scoring date, venue permission or live authority.

## Evidence classes

Classify each result as external/unreplicated, hypothesis, synthetic engineering,
explored-history diagnostic, prospective paper, registered final, or reconciled
real execution. The last class requires actual authenticated account/asset/fee
evidence; a paper simulator's fill does not qualify. Absolute net account outcome
and relative benchmark excess are different claims. Engineering passes do not
establish an edge, worldwide novelty, or permission to deploy.

Keep legacy registered verdicts unchanged. Add complementary validity/coverage
labels instead of rewriting old scores. Sparse samples and missing mandatory
fields are not estimable/incomplete rather than successful zero-result tests.

## Trial and outcome-access records

Use the offline tool described in [research-evidence-usage.md](research-evidence-usage.md).
Plan before looking at outcomes. Record trial and parent IDs; UTC plan, freeze
and outcome-access times; owner, economic mechanism, decision, stop/readout schedule, question and permitted alternatives; primary quantity;
cost/universe/control/regime definitions; source/event/availability windows;
proposer/model version; and prompt/config/code/data hashes. Record every attempted
variant, manual change, abandoned run, result and rejection. Store public-safe
hashes/summaries, not raw private prompts, protected rows or credentials.

Record outcome-visible history as explored. New files, additional runs and new
prompts cannot restore untouched status. An outcome-informed revision gets a
new version and later evaluation. Keep final evaluation outcomes outside the
development prompt for that decision. A ledger documents attestations; it cannot
prove that unreported experiments or external knowledge did not exist.

## Metric and cost contracts

Distinguish opportunities, sides, fills, completed round trips, days and shared
episodes. Declare equity/cash capital, stake, exposure and stress loss separately.
Drawdown uses a declared positive loss magnitude; reject nonfinite or inconsistent
units. Report both the all-opportunity account and any matched supported subset.

Name each cost component, payer, route/product/account/tier, size/basis, source and
validity interval. Separate modeled budgets, executable quotes, paper simulation
and reconciled debits. Unknown is not zero. Pool fee subdivisions are not extra
trader charges; count embedded economics once. Include applicable service/network,
rent, data, conversion, recurring costs and terminal inventory/failed exits.
Paired references use the same cash, caps, stops and accounting convention.

## Scoring and observation provenance

Scoring receipts must record registration and execution commits, exact scorer and
dependency hashes, data/input manifests, invocation and official window. New
source pins are prospective records, not proof of original historical identity.
Unexpected drift and failed acquisition must be explicit; never silently re-pin.
Keep primary estimator/constants/window unchanged and retain failure receipts.

Observation receipts distinguish no signal, intentionally unavailable data and
incomplete evaluation by coin/time. Record missing lookback/entry data and source
status, actual clocks and omitted reasons. An emitted intent is not a broker fill.
Late recovery cannot erase the original decision-time gap or fabricate an order.
Legacy periods without receipts remain unknown. Readiness cannot infer complete
coverage from an empty ledger or a green absence-of-errors check.
