# Candidate: participant composition and volume

**Planning only | 2026-10-14 | Owner: CoinHunt research; independent draft by Codex's Lin reviewer.** This is neither a strategy registration nor permission to acquire data, score returns, reopen a closed memecoin lane, or change frozen SLR/CAPIT studies.

**Question.** Does observable participant composition add out-of-sample economic information beyond predecision volume, price action and executable liquidity? Lin's synthetic-market study motivates an interaction question; the volume, disagreement and participation literature predates it. This is prior-art-informed research, not a novelty or return claim. No wallet is labeled rational, intelligent or profitable.

**Stage A: validate descriptive features using eligible saved evidence.** Fix the exact mint, pool/venue, feature window and decision cutoff before evaluation. Use only transactions and social observations available and sufficiently confirmed by that cutoff; retain event time, first-observed time, source coverage and parser identity. Reconcile instruction-aware gross buy debit and fee meanings. For wallet w, let B_w be observed gross buy debit in one declared quote unit; then V = sum(B_w), s_w = B_w/V, HHI = sum(s_w²), and effective buyer count = 1/HHI when V is positive and coverage supports the denominator. Report top-wallet notional shares and unique buyer counts separately.

Define a returning buyer by a verified exact-mint buy before the prospectively fixed feature-window start. Returning notional share is sum(B_w for verified returning wallets)/V. Distinguish known returning, first observed within bounded history, and history unknown; first observed does not establish a genuinely new participant. Report history bounds and uncertainty. Addresses are not people; collapse only evidenced links and retain sensitivity bounds for uncertain overlap. The previously reported 97.222% returning share and 87.354% largest-buyer share concern **one explored episode**: they cannot determine a threshold, estimate generality or supply independent validation.

For existing eligible exact-mint social observations, distinguish original authors/claims from quotes, reposts and repeated templates, preserving lineage and first-observed clocks. Report original-author breadth and repetition alongside coverage and exposure denominators. Unknown coverage is not zero activity; similar text is not proof of coordination. Validate deduplication, classification and temporal availability before any outcome analysis.

**Stage B: a separate future prospective economic candidate, only after its evidence and review prerequisites are met.** Compare a fixed volume/price/liquidity baseline with one prespecified composition interaction; use social originality as a separately declared diagnostic or separately budgeted hypothesis. Choose windows, direction, transformations, endpoint, missing-data disposition and model-search family prospectively. No future P&L, later wallet reputation, later posts, surviving-token selection or postdecision liquidity may define features. Compare complete-account net-dollar outcomes against controls with the same funding, opportunity set, venue eligibility, stake/exposure limits, observation clocks and execution assumptions; prespecify control scheduling without selecting future favorable entries. Reject promotion if fresh evidence adds no economically meaningful benefit at declared costs, or the apparent benefit depends on later information, one event or the largest buyer.

**Prerequisites and power decision.** Require an authorized untouched cohort, continuous exact-mint chronology/social coverage, executable entry/exit quotes at intended size and latency, and matched full-account controls. Count embedded pool/protocol/creator/buyback economics once; retain separately applicable service, network, rent, data and conversion costs and distinguish configured fees from measured commission. Unvalued residual inventory, missing exits or failed sources prevent complete profit claims. US-usable route, rounding, capacity and existing authorization gates remain separate requirements. Plan power and uncertainty around independent mint/event/launch-day and common-market clusters, with repeated-wallet overlap handled explicitly—not swap or post counts. Before evaluation, justify the attainable sample and meaningful net-dollar effect using permitted training evidence and the full search budget. Insufficient coverage, power or economics means **INCOMPLETE / do not advance**. No acquisition, return simulation, registration or strategy edit is authorized by this proposal.
